# GnuCash Persian Accounting Handbook

> **Built by the community, for the community.**

**GnuCash Persian Accounting Handbook** is a community-driven open-source project dedicated to making GnuCash a first-class accounting solution for Persian-speaking users and organizations.

The project provides comprehensive documentation, localization resources, accounting guidance, taxation references, templates, plugins, development tools, and real-world examples to help individuals, businesses, educators, accountants, and developers effectively use and extend GnuCash.

> **This project is community-driven and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by the GnuCash Project.**

---

# 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to build the most comprehensive open-source Persian resource for GnuCash by combining accounting knowledge, localization, software development, and community collaboration into a single, freely accessible handbook.

---

# 🌍 Vision

To make GnuCash the preferred open-source accounting solution for Persian-speaking users and organizations by providing high-quality documentation, practical accounting guidance, localization resources, and community-driven development.

---

# ❤️ Core Values

## 🤝 Community First

We believe the best projects are built through collaboration, openness, and mutual respect. Everyone is welcome to contribute.

## 📖 Open Knowledge

Knowledge should be freely available. Our documentation, examples, and educational resources are created to benefit everyone.

## ⚖️ Accuracy

We strive to provide technically correct, well-documented, and verifiable information. Whenever possible, accounting practices are supported by accounting standards, regulations, official documentation, or other reliable references.

## 🌍 Localization

We aim to make GnuCash feel natural and practical for Persian-speaking users while respecting internationally accepted accounting principles.

## 💼 Practical Accounting

We focus on real-world accounting workflows rather than theory alone by providing practical examples, templates, and case studies.

## 🔍 Transparency

Our decisions, discussions, documentation, and development process are open to the community.

## 🚀 Continuous Improvement

This handbook evolves through community feedback, software improvements, regulatory changes, and practical experience.
